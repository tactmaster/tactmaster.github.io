Preheat oven to 200°C

Ingredients:

| ![Vegetable stock](/img/vegetablestock.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Water](/img/water.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Kale](/img/kale.jpg){:height="100px"} |
| 1 vegetable stock cube | 450 ml water | Kale or spinach |
| ![Broccoli](/img/broccoli.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Bulgur wheat](/img/bulgurwheat.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Mozzarella](/img/mozzarella.jpg){:height="100px"} |
| Frozen broccoli | 160 g bulgur wheat | 2 balls mozzarella |

Method:
1. Make vegetable stock with 1 stock cube and 450 ml water.
2. Strip kale or spinach leaves off the stalks.
3. Dump kale or spinach leaves, broccoli, bulgur wheat, stock and 1 ball of chopped mozzarella into an oven dish and stir.
4. Bake for 20 minutes.
5. *Stir*, then cut the other mozzarella ball into circles and put them on top.
6. Bake for 15 minutes more.
