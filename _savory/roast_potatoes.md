Preheat oven to 180°C

Ingredients:

| ![Oil](/img/oil.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Potatoes](/img/kingedwardpotatoes.jpg){:height="100px"} |  |
| Oil to cover the bottom of the tin | 1 kg floury potatoes, e.g. King Edward (mjölig potatis) |  |

Method:
1. Pour oil into a roasting tin so it covers the bottom and comes a little bit up the sides.
2. Put the tin in the oven so the oil gets hot.
3. Peel the potatoes and cut them into medium-sized pieces.
4. Boil until they are a little bit soft on the edges. Peeling, cutting and boiling takes about 15-20 minutes, so the oil will be hot by then.
5. Drain, then gently shake the pan to make the outsides of the potatoes fluffy.
6. Carefully put the potatoes into the hot oil (it will spit!).
7. Baste the potatoes so they are nicely covered in oil.
8. Roast for 20 minutes.
9. Baste again.
10. Roast for 20 minutes more (40 minutes in total).
