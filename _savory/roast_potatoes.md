Preheat oven to 180°C

Ingredients:

| ![Oil](/img/oil.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Potatoes](/img/kingedwardpotatoes.jpg){:height="100px"} |  |
| Oil | 1 kg floury potatoes, e.g. King Edward (mjölig potatis) |  |

Method:
1. Pour oil into a roasting tin so it covers the bottom.
2. Put the roasting tin in the oven.
3. Peel the potatoes and cut them into medium-sized pieces.
4. Boil until they are a little bit soft on the edges.
5. Drain, then gently shake the pan to make the outsides fluffy.
6. Put the potatoes in the roasting tin.
7. Baste the potatoes.
8. Roast for 20 minutes.
9. Baste again.
10. Roast for 20 minutes more.
