Preheat oven to 180°C

| ![Oil](/img/oil.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Turkish yoghurt](/img/turkishyoghurt.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Honey](/img/honey.jpg){:height="100px"} |
| 80 ml (6 tablespoons) oil | 125 g Turkish yoghurt | 1 tablespoon honey |
| ![Blueberries](/img/blueberries.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Flour](/img/flour.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Baking powder](/img/bakingpowder.jpg){:height="100px"} |
| 40 g blueberries | 130 g flour | 1.25 teaspoon baking powder |
| ![Bicarb](/img/bicarb.jpg){:height="100px"} |  |  |
| 0.25 teaspoons bicarb |  |  |

Method:
1. Mix oil, Turkish yoghurt and honey.
2. Mix in blueberries, flour, baking powder and bicarb.
3. Put into 8 muffin cases and bake for around 20 minutes.
