Preheat oven to 180°C

For brownies

| ![Butter](/img/butter.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Dark chocolate](/img/darkchocolate.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Egg](/img/egg.jpg){:height="100px"} |
| 50 g butter | 50 g dark chocolate | 1 egg |
| ![Sugar](/img/sugar.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Flour](/img/flour.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Baking powder](/img/bakingpowder.jpg){:height="100px"} |
| 85 g sugar | 15 g flour | 0.25 teaspoon baking powder |
| ![Cocoa](/img/cocoa.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Vanilla](/img/vanilla.jpg){:height="100px"} |  |
| 20 g cocoa powder | 0.25 teaspoon vanilla |  |

For cookies

| ![Butter](/img/butter.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Sugar](/img/sugar.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Brown sugar](/img/brownsugar.jpg){:height="100px"} |
| 38 g butter | 1 tablespoon sugar | 2 tablespoons brown sugar |
| ![Egg](/img/egg.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Flour](/img/flour.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Bicarb](/img/bicarb.jpg){:height="100px"} |
| 1/8 egg | 3/4 dl flour | 1/8 teaspoon bicarb |
| ![Baking powder](/img/bakingpowder.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Vanilla](/img/vanilla.jpg){:height="100px"} | ![Chocolate chips](/img/chocchips.jpg){:height="100px"} |
| 1/8 teaspoon baking powder | 1/8 teaspoon vanilla | 30 g chocolate chips |

Method:
1. Melt butter and chocolate for brownies together.
2. Mix with all other brownie ingredients.
3. Mix all cookie ingredients together.
4. Line a square glass Ikea box with baking paper.
5. Pour in most of the brownie batter (leave some left over) then squish most of the cookie dough on top (leave some left over). Eat excess mixtures!
6. Bake for around 30 minutes.
